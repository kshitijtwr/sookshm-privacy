# Sookshm Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 November 2026_

Sookshm ("the app") compresses and resizes photos, signatures, PDFs and videos on your phone.

## Your files stay on your device
All processing happens on your device. The app has no accounts and no backend, and it never uploads your photos, signatures, PDFs, videos or any other file. Temporary working copies are kept in the app's private storage and deleted when a job ends.

## Permissions
- **Camera:** only when you choose to take a photo inside the app. Pictures are not sent anywhere.
- **Microphone:** only when you tap Record for a voice-over in the video editor, and only while it is recording. The recording is saved in the app's private storage as part of your project and is never uploaded. You can turn the permission off in Android settings at any time.
- **Photos and files:** you pick files with the Android system picker. The app does not request broad storage or media access.
- When saving, location data (EXIF GPS in photos, location tags in videos) is removed from the output by default.

## Third-party services
Only the services below receive any data from the app, and none of it is your files.

- **Google AdMob** shows ads in the free version. The AdMob SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (which can indicate a general location), how you use the app (for example app launches and taps on ads), and diagnostic information (for example launch time and crashes). This is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent before loading ads. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **RevenueCat** handles premium purchases. It receives your purchase and subscription history and an anonymous app user ID, used to unlock premium features and to restore purchases. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **Firebase (Google)** provides crash reports, usage statistics and push notifications.
  - **Crashlytics** receives crash and error reports with basic device information (for example phone model, Android version and a Firebase installation ID) so we can fix crashes.
  - **Analytics** receives which screens are opened and general usage counts. It is switched off until you give consent where consent is required, and it never includes your files, file names or content.
  - **Cloud Messaging** delivers notifications from us (for example news about a new version). It uses a token that identifies the app on your phone, and your app language so we can send the right language. You can turn notifications off in Android settings at any time; the app asks for the permission once on Android 13 and later.
  See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Google ML Kit** cuts out the subject of a photo when you use Cutout in the editors. Your photos are processed on your phone and are not sent anywhere. ML Kit does send Google diagnostic and usage data, such as your phone model and Android version, the app's name and version, an identifier for this installation that is not meant to identify you or your device, performance figures and error codes. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
- Payments are processed by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data.
